Joining Busiada Girls

How to Join Grade 10

A simple guide for parents and learners.

01

Complete Grade 9

Your child finishes Junior School and sits for the KJSEA examination.

Results can be checked on the KNEC website.

02

Select Pathway and Schools

Together with teachers, choose one pathway, subject combination, and eight senior schools.

The Junior School submits the choices through the official portal:

https://selection-placement.kemis.go.ke

03

Ministry Placement

The Ministry of Education places learners into Senior Schools based on performance and the choices made.

04

Check Placement Result

Find out the school your child has been placed in by:

  • Using the online portal: https://placement.education.go.ke
  • Or sending the Assessment Number via SMS to 22263
05

Download Admission Letter

Download the official joining letter from the same portal:

https://placement.education.go.ke

This is the only valid admission letter.

06

Report to School

Bring your child to the placed school on the official reporting dates with the admission letter and required documents.

07

Complete Admission

The school will officially admit the learner after she reports.

More Official Information

Ministry of Education: https://www.education.go.ke

Important Note: Always use the official government portals. Placement is done by the Ministry of Education, not by individual schools.
