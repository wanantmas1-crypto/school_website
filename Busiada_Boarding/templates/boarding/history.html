Our History

The story of Busiada Girls’ High School — from humble beginnings to a centre of excellence.

A journey of growth

Building a legacy, one generation at a time

Busiada Girls’ High School has grown from a modest institution into one of the respected girls’ schools in Busia County. Rooted in the values of the Anglican Church of Kenya, the school has remained committed to academic excellence, discipline, and the holistic development of every girl.

Over the years, the school has built a strong culture of hard work, sisterhood, and service. Today, Busiada continues to nurture confident young women who are ready to unleash their potential and contribute meaningfully to society.
