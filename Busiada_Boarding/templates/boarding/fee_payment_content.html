At school

Cash Payment

Cash payments are discouraged. Please use M-Pesa or bank transfer whenever possible. If paying by cash is unavoidable, pay only at the school accounts office during working hours and collect an official receipt.

Important Instructions for Parents

  • Always use the student’s Admission Number as the payment reference.
  • Keep your M-Pesa confirmation message or bank slip safely.
  • Present the payment confirmation at the school accounts office if required.
  • For payment issues, contact the school office.
