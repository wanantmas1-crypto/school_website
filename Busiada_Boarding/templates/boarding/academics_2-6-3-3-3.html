Why CBE Matters

Learning that turns knowledge into ability.

CBE focuses on what a learner can do, not just what she can remember. It helps every girl discover her strengths, develop useful skills, and prepare for university, TVET, employment, or entrepreneurship.

Learners choose a pathway based on interest and ability.

Skills, values, and character are emphasized.

Learning prepares girls for real life and further education.

Talent development goes hand in hand with academics.

Pillars of CBE at Busiada

What we stand on.

  • Pathway Choice — Every girl selects a path that matches her strengths.
  • Skill Development — Focus on practical and useful competencies.
  • Character Formation — Values and discipline are part of daily learning.
  • Talent Nurturing — Arts, sports, and leadership are fully supported.
  • Future Readiness — Preparation for university, TVET, work, and life.

What Parents Should Know

CBE allows early specialisation through pathways.

Learners still take core subjects: English, Kiswahili, PE, and Community Service Learning.

The system values both academic ability and practical skills.

Success is measured by competence, character, and readiness for the next stage.
